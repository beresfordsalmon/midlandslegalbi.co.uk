Every figure is checked before it reaches you

  1. 01Reconciled. Totals must agree on every slide, with the source data, and with your own backup workbook.
  2. 02Blocked if wrong. If a check fails, the pack can't be sent. I see a clearly marked preview and fix the problem first.
  3. 03Flagged, not hidden. If your case-management system has something wrong, such as a live case showing a zero balance, I list it for your team to correct rather than quietly patching the report.
