About me

I started in credit control, chasing aged debt and reporting on it to managers, directors and clients. At one firm I cut a £250,000 aged-debt ledger to £11,000 in six months. In 2023 I joined a law firm's debt recovery team, setting up debtor accounts and running case files.

That's where the reporting started. Since July 2023 I've produced a UK law firm's monthly and quarterly debt recovery reporting, and I haven't missed a deadline.

So I know what the figures mean as well as how to build them: what a month's balance movement is telling you, why cases sit waiting for instructions, and what your client will ask about first. You deal with one person, on a fixed monthly fee, and your pack arrives on the same day every month.
